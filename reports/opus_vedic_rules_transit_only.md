## SECTION A — PANCHANGA: THE FIVE LIMBS OF TIME (Pure Sun-Moon Math)

**1. Tithi (Lunar Day)**
The angular distance between Moon and Sun divided into 30 units of 12° each; computed as (Moon longitude − Sun longitude)/12. Completely natal-independent and the single most tested variable in Indian market astrology.

**2. Shukla Paksha (Waxing Fortnight)**
The 15 tithis from Amavasya to Purnima where the Moon gains light. Traditionally the phase of accumulation, expansion, rising liquidity, and bullish bias.

**3. Krishna Paksha (Waning Fortnight)**
The 15 tithis from Purnima to Amavasya where the Moon loses light. Traditionally the phase of distribution, contraction, profit-taking, and bearish bias.

**4. Amavasya (New Moon)**
Sun and Moon in exact conjunction (0° elongation). A void/nil point associated with indecision, low volume, hidden accumulation, and frequent trend-reversal pivots.

**5. Purnima (Full Moon)**
Sun and Moon in exact opposition (180° elongation). Associated with emotional extremes, volume spikes, blow-off tops, panic bottoms, and maximum volatility.

**6. Ashtami Tithi (8th Lunar Day)**
Moon at 84°–96° or 264°–276° from Sun (first-quarter and last-quarter squares). Classified as Rikta-adjacent/harsh; historically correlated with mid-cycle breakdowns and sharp corrections.

**7. Chaturdashi Tithi (14th Lunar Day)**
The tithi immediately preceding both Purnima and Amavasya. Considered highly agitated and fear-driven; a common day for capitulation candles.

**8. Rikta Tithis (Empty Tithis — 4, 9, 14)**
Chaturthi, Navami and Chaturdashi are called "empty/void" tithis, denied for all auspicious commencement. Test as loss/decline days for fresh long entries.

**9. Nanda Tithis (Joyful — 1, 6, 11)**
Pratipada, Shashti, Ekadashi — associated with joy, new beginnings, and growth. Test as favorable initiation days.

**10. Bhadra Tithis (Auspicious — 2, 7, 12)**
Dwitiya, Saptami, Dwadashi — associated with stability and welfare; test as consolidation/steady-uptrend days.

**11. Jaya Tithis (Victory — 3, 8, 13)**
Tritiya, Ashtami, Trayodashi — associated with conflict and victory-through-struggle; test as high-volatility trend days.

**12. Purna Tithis (Full/Complete — 5, 10, 15/30)**
Panchami, Dashami, Purnima/Amavasya — associated with completion and fullness; test as culmination/exhaustion days.

**13. Tithi Lords (Tithi Swami)**
Each tithi has a ruling deity/planet (e.g., Pratipada–Agni, Dwitiya–Brahma, Chaturthi–Ganesha/Yama, Shashti–Kartikeya, Ashtami–Rudra/Shiva, Navami–Durga, Chaturdashi–Kali/Shiva). Group tithis by lord and test aggregate returns per lord.

**14. Kshaya Tithi (Lost/Skipped Tithi)**
When the Moon moves so fast that a tithi begins and ends between two sunrises, it is "lost" from the calendar. Traditionally denotes a missing/vanishing quality — test for gap-down days and sudden liquidity holes.

**15. Adhika Tithi / Vriddhi Tithi (Repeated Tithi)**
When the Moon is slow and a tithi spans two sunrises, it repeats. Denotes stagnation and delay — test for range-bound, inside-bar sessions.

**16. Dagdha Tithi (Burnt Tithi)**
Specific tithi–weekday combinations rendered "burnt" (e.g., Dwitiya on Sunday/Tuesday, Chaturthi on Monday/Friday, Shashti on Thursday, Ashtami on Wednesday, Dashami on Sunday, Dwadashi on Tuesday, Chaturdashi on Saturday). Classic loss-and-destruction signature.

**17. Vaar (Weekday) & Its Planetary Lord**
Sunday–Sun, Monday–Moon, Tuesday–Mars, Wednesday–Mercury, Thursday–Jupiter, Friday–Venus, Saturday–Saturn. The purest natal-free variable; test day-of-week effects as *planetary* effects, not calendar effects.

**18. Vaar-Based Sector Bias**
Because each weekday carries its lord's significations, test Tuesday (Mars) for energy/metals/defense volatility, Friday (Venus) for luxury/autos/finance, Thursday (Jupiter) for banking/expansion, Saturday–Sunday closures for accumulated Saturnine pressure into Monday.

**19. Nakshatra of the Moon (Chandra Nakshatra)**
Moon's position among the 27 lunar mansions (each 13°20′). The Moon changes nakshatra roughly daily, giving a natural 27-day cycle to test against index returns.

**20. Nakshatra Lords (Vimshottari Dasha Lords as Daily Flavour)**
Each nakshatra is ruled by one of nine grahas (Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, repeating thrice). Group all trading days by Moon's nakshatra lord and compute mean return, hit-rate, and volatility per lord.

**21. Nakshatra Gana (Deva / Manushya / Rakshasa)**
Nakshatras are classified as divine, human, or demonic in temperament. Test Rakshasa-gana Moon days (Krittika, Ashlesha, Magha, Chitra, Vishakha, Jyeshtha, Moola, Dhanishtha, Shatabhisha) for aggression, gaps, and panic.

**22. Nakshatra Nature (Chara, Sthira, Ugra, Mishra, Kshipra, Mridu, Tikshna)**
Movable, fixed, fierce, mixed, swift, tender, and sharp nakshatras. Test "Tikshna/Ugra" Moon days (Ardra, Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Moola, Bharani, Magha, Purva Phalguni, Purvashadha, Purva Bhadrapada) for crashes and sharp reversals.

**23. Nakshatra Tattva (Element)**
Each nakshatra carries an elemental assignment; Agni (fire) nakshatras correlate with energy/inflation spikes, Jala (water) with liquidity and commodities, Vayu (air) with communication/tech and volatility, Prithvi (earth) with real estate and metals.

**29. Malefic Nitya Yogas (The Nine Denied Yogas)**
Vishkambha (1), Atiganda (6), Shoola (9), Ganda (10), Vyaghata (13), Vajra (15), Vyatipata (17), Parigha (19), Vaidhriti (27) are traditionally rejected. Test these nine specifically for negative index returns.

**30. Vyatipata Yoga (17th Nitya Yoga)**
A "great calamity" yoga; also occurs astronomically when Sun and Moon have equal declination on opposite sides of the equator. Classic accident/disaster/crash signature.

**31. Vaidhriti Yoga (27th Nitya Yoga)**
The "holding apart" yoga; astronomically when Sun and Moon have equal declination on the same side. Denotes rupture, separation, and breakdown of agreements — test for gap-downs and policy shocks.

**32. Amrita, Siddha, Shubha, Priti, Ayushman, Saubhagya, Shobhana Yogas**
The most benevolent Nitya Yogas (nos. 2, 4, 5, 21, 3, 8, ...). Test as favorable-accumulation markers.

**33. Karana (Half-Tithi — 11 Karanas, 60 per Lunar Month)**
Each tithi splits into two karanas of 6° elongation each; seven movable karanas (Bava, Balava, Kaulava, Taitila, Gara, Vanija, Vishti) rotate eight times, plus four fixed (Shakuni, Chatushpada, Naga, Kimstughna).

**34. Vishti / Bhadra Karana**
The most feared karana, occurring roughly 8 times per month for ~half a tithi. Universally denied for beginnings — a prime candidate for testing intraday and daily declines.

**35. Vanija Karana (The Merchant)**
The karana of trade, commerce, and profit, ruled by trading activity itself. Test explicitly for volume expansion and profitable long entries.

**36. Shakuni, Chatushpada, Naga, Kimstughna (Fixed Karanas)**
These four occur only once per lunar month around Amavasya. Naga and Chatushpada are malefic (obstruction, loss), Kimstughna mildly benefic — cluster them around the New Moon pivot.

**37. Panchaka Rahita (Five-Fold Defect Removal)**
A composite scoring of Tithi, Vaar, Nakshatra, Lagna and Yoga defects; the natal-free portion (Tithi/Vaar/Nakshatra/Yoga) yields Mrityu-Panchaka, Agni-Panchaka, Raja-Panchaka, Chora-Panchaka and Roga-Panchaka. Test Mrityu-Panchaka and Chora-Panchaka (theft/loss) days for drawdowns.

**38. Tithi–Vaar Yogas: Siddha Yoga**
Specific auspicious tithi-weekday pairs (e.g., Nanda tithi on Friday, Bhadra tithi on Wednesday, Jaya tithi on Tuesday, Rikta tithi on Saturday, Purna tithi on Thursday). Test as high-probability success days.

**39. Tithi–Vaar Yogas: Mrityu Yoga**
Deadly tithi-weekday pairs (e.g., Nanda on Saturday, Bhadra on Thursday, Jaya on Monday, Rikta on Friday, Purna on Wednesday, per classical tables). Test for crash clustering.

**40. Nakshatra–Vaar Yogas: Amrita Siddha Yoga**
Auspicious nakshatra-weekday pairs (Sunday–Hasta, Monday–Mrigashira, Tuesday–Ashwini, Wednesday–Anuradha, Thursday–Pushya, Friday–Revati, Saturday–Rohini). Extremely strong "growth" signature.

**41. Nakshatra–Vaar Yogas: Sarvartha Siddhi Yoga**
A larger set of nakshatra-weekday combinations granting all-round accomplishment (e.g., Sunday with Ashwini/Pushya/Hasta/Moola/Uttara trio; Monday with Rohini/Mrigashira/Pushya/Anuradha/Shravana). Test as bullish continuation days.

**42. Nakshatra–Vaar Yogas: Mrityu Yoga (Nakshatra Version)**
Fatal pairs such as Sunday–Anuradha, Monday–Uttarashadha, Tuesday–Shatabhisha, Wednesday–Ashwini, Thursday–Mrigashira, Friday–Ashlesha, Saturday–Hasta. Test as high-risk sessions.

**43. Nakshatra–Vaar Yogas: Yamaghata Yoga**
Sunday–Magha, Monday–Vishakha, Tuesday–Ardra, Wednesday–Moola, Thursday–Krittika, Friday–Rohini, Saturday–Uttarashadha. Denotes obstruction and loss of effort.

**44. Utpata / Mrityu / Kaana / Siddhi Yogas (Tithi-Nakshatra Combos)**
Additional classical composite yogas from tithi-nakshatra pairings, indicating omen, death, blindness (to risk), and accomplishment respectively.

**45. Dwipushkar Yoga (Double-Fruit Yoga)**
Formed when a Bhadra tithi (2, 7, 12) + specific weekday (Sunday, Tuesday, Saturday) + a two-pada nakshatra (Vishakha, Krittika, Uttarashadha, Purvashadha, Uttara Phalguni, Purva Bhadrapada) coincide. Whatever happens repeats/doubles — test for range-expansion and follow-through days.

**46. Tripushkar Yoga (Triple-Fruit Yoga)**
Same tithi/weekday rule with three-pada nakshatras (Krittika, Punarvasu, Uttara Phalguni, Vishakha, Uttarashadha, Purva Bhadrapada). Gains or losses triple — an excellent volatility-expansion test.

**47. Ravi Yoga**
When the Moon's nakshatra is a specific count from the Sun's nakshatra (4, 6, 9, 10, 13, 20 per some texts), all doshas are destroyed. Purely Sun-Moon nakshatra arithmetic.

**48. Hutashana Yoga, Krakacha Yoga, Danda Yoga, Kaala Yoga**
Additional tithi-vaar-nakshatra composite malefic yogas denoting burning, sawing (cutting), punishment, and death. Each is a distinct rule-based boolean flag.

**49. Visha Ghati / Vish Ghatika**
A fixed toxic sub-period within each nakshatra (specified in ghatis from nakshatra start), varying per nakshatra. Useful for intraday backtests on high-frequency data.

**50. Amrita Ghati**
The nectar sub-period within each nakshatra, opposite in effect to Vish Ghati. Test for intraday lows/reversal entries.

## SECTION B — LUNAR ASTRONOMY (Absolute, Location-Free)

**51. Moon's Sign Ingress (Chandra Rashi Parivartan)**
The Moon changes sign every ~2.25 days, giving 12–13 ingresses per month. Test the ingress day and the sign-tenancy period separately.

**52. Moon in Movable / Fixed / Dual Signs (Chara / Sthira / Dwiswabhava)**
Moon in Chara signs (Ar, Cn, Li, Cp) for trend initiation and volatility; Sthira (Ta, Le, Sc, Aq) for consolidation and stubborn trends; Dwiswabhava (Ge, Vi, Sg, Pi) for whipsaws and reversals.

**53. Moon in Odd/Even, Male/Female Signs**
Krura (odd/male) signs vs. Saumya (even/female) signs. Test directional bias of the index by Moon's sign parity.

**54. Moon's Elongation Bands (Paksha Bala)**
The Moon's strength increases linearly from 0° to 180° elongation. Compute a continuous 0–1 "Paksha Bala" score for every trading day and regress against returns — a superior alternative to binary Shukla/Krishna.

**55. Balachandra / Kshina Chandra (Weak Moon)**
Moon within 72° of the Sun (i.e., Pratipada to Panchami of Shukla, and Ekadashi to Amavasya of Krishna) is classified as weak/debilitated by light. Test as fear/low-confidence periods.

**56. Purna Chandra (Strong Moon)**
Moon beyond 120° elongation is fully strong. Test as confidence/liquidity-rich periods.

**57. Moon's Perigee & Apogee (Chandra Nikatasthiti)**
Moon's distance from Earth varies ~356,000–406,000 km on a ~27.55-day anomalistic cycle. Perigee (fast Moon, higher tides) tends to correlate with volatility expansion; test both extremes.

**58. Supermoon / Micromoon (Perigee-Syzygy)**
When Purnima or Amavasya coincides with perigee. Historically a favored crash/spike marker in financial astrology literature — a compound flag worth isolating.

**59. Moon's Maximum Declination (Northern & Southern Extremes)**
The Moon reaches ~±23°–28° declination twice each month. Extreme declination is a classic W.D. Gann/Vedic hybrid turning-point marker; test both max-north and max-south dates.

**60. Moon Crossing the Celestial Equator (Zero Declination)**
Twice per month the Moon crosses 0° declination. Test as short-cycle inflection dates.

**61. Lunar Nodal Standst**61. Lunar Nodal Standstill / Nodal Extremes (18.6-Year Cycle)**
The lunar nodes complete one retrograde revolution in ~18.6 years, producing "major" and "minor" lunar standstills in declination range. Test the 18.6-year and 9.3-year harmonic dates against major secular index turning points.

**62. Moon's Speed (Chandra Gati — Sighra / Manda)**
The Moon's daily motion varies from ~11°45′ to ~15°25′. Test "fast Moon" days (>14°) for trend acceleration and "slow Moon" days (<12°30′) for stalling and reversal.

**63. Void-of-Course Moon (Vedic Adaptation)**
Period when the Moon forms no further exact Drishti to any planet before changing sign. Traditionally denotes actions that "go nowhere" — test for low-volume, directionless sessions and failed breakouts.

**64. Moon's Latitude Crossings (Bhukti/Vikshepa)**
Moon's ecliptic latitude swings ±5°. Zero-latitude crossings coincide with node passage; extreme latitude days can be tested independently.

**65. Chandrashtama (Moon in the 8th from a Reference Sign)**
Though normally natal, this becomes natal-free if you use the *mundane* reference of the current Solar sign, or the market's ruling nakshatra sign. Test Moon transiting the 8th from the Sun's sign as a stress marker.

**66. Moon–Sun Nakshatra Distance (Tara / Nakshatra Count)**
Count from Sun's nakshatra to Moon's nakshatra, mod 9, giving Janma, Sampat, Vipat, Kshema, Pratyari, Sadhaka, Vadha, Mitra, Ati-Mitra. Test Vipat (3), Pratyari (5) and Vadha (7) days for losses — this is a natal-free "solar Tarabala."

**67. Moon's Nakshatra Pada Transit (108-Fold Daily Grid)**
Moon traverses one pada in ~8 hours, giving 3 padas per day. Extremely useful for intraday backtests on 108 discrete zones.

**68. Sarvatobhadra Chakra — Nakshatra Vedha (Piercing)**
An absolute grid in which the day's nakshatra is "pierced" (vedha) by planets occupying specific counter-nakshatras. Requires only transit longitudes; test vedha-days for adverse outcomes.

**69. Kota Chakra (Fort Diagram) Transits**
The 28 nakshatras are arranged as a fort with Stambha (pillar), Madhya (interior), Prakara (rampart), and Bahya (exterior). Malefics entering the Stambha or crossing inward, and the Kota Swami/Kota Pala positions, are classic siege/decline markers.

**70. Sapta Nadi Chakra (Seven-Channel Diagram)**
The 27 nakshatras arranged in 7 columns/channels; planets falling in the same nadi produce compression and extreme events (drought, crash, deluge). Purely nakshatra-based and natal-free.

**71. Tri-Nadi Chakra (Prachanda / Chanda / Vata)**
A three-channel variant used in rainfall and commodity forecasting; planetary clustering in one nadi signals extremity in prices.

## SECTION C — SOLAR TRANSITS & THE SOLAR YEAR

**72. Surya Sankranti (Solar Ingress into a Sidereal Sign)**
The Sun's entry into each of the 12 rasis, occurring ~monthly. Each Sankranti moment is a mundane "month chart" trigger; test the ingress date ±3 days for turning points.

**73. Mesha Sankranti / Sidereal Aries Ingress (Vedic Solar New Year)**
The Sun's entry into 0° sidereal Aries — the single most important mundane annual marker (Varsha Pravesh of the world). Test annual index behavior anchored to this date.

**74. Makara Sankranti (Uttarayana Begins)**
Sun enters sidereal Capricorn; start of the northward (Uttarayana) course. Traditionally the "devas' day," associated with expansion, optimism, and growth phases.

**75. Karka Sankranti (Dakshinayana Begins)**
Sun enters sidereal Cancer; start of the southward course. Traditionally the "pitris' night," associated with contraction, introspection, and defensive markets.

**76. Ayana Sandhi (Solstitial Junctions)**
The transition zones at Uttarayana/Dakshinayana onset. Test as high-volatility regime-change windows.

**77. Vishuva (Equinoctial Sankrantis — Aries & Libra Ingress)**
Sidereal equinoctial ingresses; classical texts treat these as balance-points where price equilibrium breaks. Test for trend reversals.

**78. Shadashiti Mukha / Sankranti Punya Kala Classification**
Each Sankranti is classified by the weekday and time it occurs, yielding names such as Mandā, Mandākinī, Dhwānkshī, Ghorā, Mahodari, and their predicted effects on grain prices, kings, and merchants. This is *the* classical mundane price-forecast rule.

**79. Sankranti Weekday Rule (Vaar of Ingress)**
The weekday on which the Sun changes sign determines the prosperity or scarcity of the ensuing month per Brihat Samhita. A directly testable annual/monthly rule.

**80. Sun's Nakshatra Transit (Surya Nakshatra — 27-Fold Solar Year)**
The Sun spends ~13.5 days in each nakshatra, giving a 27-part solar year. Test index seasonality by Sun's nakshatra rather than by Gregorian month.

**81. Ardra Pravesha (Sun Enters Ardra)**
The Sun's entry into Ardra nakshatra (~June 22), classically the monsoon-onset chart for the Indian subcontinent and the primary agricultural/commodity price forecast moment.

**82. Rohini Vasa / Rohini Yoga (Sun's Passage & Moon in Rohini)**
Classical rainfall/harvest prediction using Moon-in-Rohini conditions during Jyeshtha month. Directly relevant to agricultural commodity backtests.

**83. Sun's Passage Through Mrigashira, Ardra, Punarvasu, Pushya, Ashlesha, Magha (Nautapa)**
The nine hottest days when Sun is in early Rohini/Mrigashira; classical heat and price-rise indicator. Testable annually.

**84. Sun in Gandanta / Rasi Sandhi**
Sun crossing the last degree of a water sign into a fire sign, or any sign-junction. Test as monthly micro-inflection.

**85. Sun's Northern/Southern Declination Bands**
Sun's declination cycle ±23°26′. Test index behavior in declination bands (e.g., >+20°, 0° to +20°, 0° to −20°, <−20°) as a pure astronomical seasonality proxy.

**86. Abda / Samvatsara (60-Year Jovian-Solar Cycle)**
The named 60-year cycle (Prabhava through Akshaya). Names such as **Kshaya** (loss/decay), **Durmukhi** (ugly-faced), **Roudri** (fierce), **Krodhi** (angry), **Dundubhi**, **Rakshasa**, **Vyaya** (expenditure/loss) carry explicit prosperity or scarcity verdicts. Map historical years to samvatsara names and test aggregate index returns.

**87. Samvatsara Adhipati (Lord of the Year)**
The planet ruling the year, determined by the weekday of Mesha Sankranti. A Saturn- or Mars-ruled year is classically a scarcity/conflict year; Jupiter/Venus years are abundance years.

**88. Ugadi Ministers — Raja, Mantri, Senadhipati, Sasyadhipati, Dhanyadhipati, Rasadhipati, Nirasadhipati, Meghadhipati, Arghadhipati**
Nine annual "ministers" assigned by fixed rules from the New Year moment. **Arghadhipati** literally means *Lord of Prices* and **Dhanyadhipati** *Lord of Grains* — the most explicitly financial mundane rule in the entire corpus. Test years by Arghadhipati planet.

**89. Rasi-Based Mundane Assignment (Kurma Chakra / Desha-Vibhaga)**
Geographic regions and nations are assigned to rasis and nakshatras. For US indices, the relevant rasi/nakshatra assignment lets you test malefic transits over the *country's* zone without any birth chart.

**90. Adhika Masa (Intercalary Lunar Month)**
When two Amavasyas fall within one solar month, an extra "Mala Masa" (impure month) is inserted. Traditionally inauspicious for new ventures — test these ~32-month-recurring windows.

**91. Kshaya Masa (Lost Lunar Month)**
Extremely rare month-skipping; treated as ominous for the world. Flag historically.

**92. Shravana / Bhadrapada / Pausha / Chaitra Lunar-Month Effects**
The 12 sidereal lunar months (named by the nakshatra of the Purnima). Test index seasonality by lunar month, which drifts against the Gregorian calendar and thus separates "astro" from "calendar" seasonality.

## SECTION D — PLANETARY DIGNITY: ABSOLUTE ZODIACAL STATES

**93. Uccha (Exaltation) Degrees — Exact**
Sun 10° Aries, Moon 3° Taurus, Mars 28° Capricorn, Mercury 15° Virgo, Jupiter 5° Cancer, Venus 27° Pisces, Saturn 20° Libra, Rahu 20° Taurus (some say Gemini), Ketu 20° Scorpio (some say Sagittarius). Test the exact-degree crossing dates as maximum-strength events.

**94. Neecha (Debilitation) Degrees — Exact**
Sun 10° Libra, Moon 3° Scorpio, Mars 28° Cancer, Mercury 15° Pisces, Jupiter 5° Capricorn, Venus 27° Virgo, Saturn 20° Aries. Test exact-degree crossings for maximum-weakness/decline events.

**95. Exaltation Sign Tenancy (Whole-Sign)**
A planet anywhere in its exaltation sign. Test the entire tenancy period (e.g., Jupiter in Cancer, Saturn in Libra) against index regime.

**96. Debilitation Sign Tenancy (Whole-Sign)**
A planet anywhere in its debilitation sign — e.g., Jupiter in Capricorn (a repeatedly cited crash signature), Saturn in Aries, Venus in Virgo. Prime backtest candidates.

**97. Swakshetra (Own Sign) Transits**
Planet in its own sign: Sun–Leo, Moon–Cancer, Mars–Aries/Scorpio, Mercury–Gemini/Virgo, Jupiter–Sagittarius/Pisces, Venus–Taurus/Libra, Saturn–Capricorn/Aquarius. Test as stability/strength periods.

**98. Moolatrikona**98. Moolatrikona (Root-Triangle) Degree Zones**
Precise sub-arcs of maximum functional strength: Sun 0°–20° Leo, Moon 4°–30° Taurus, Mars 0°–12° Aries, Mercury 16°–20° Virgo, Jupiter 0°–10° Sagittarius, Venus 0°–15° Libra, Saturn 0°–20° Aquarius. Test only when the transiting planet is inside the exact arc, not merely in the sign.

**99. Mitra Kshetra / Shatru Kshetra (Friendly & Enemy Signs)**
Naisargika (natural) friendship tables determine whether a planet transits a friend's, neutral's, or enemy's sign. Build a daily "dignity score" for all nine grahas summed into a single index — a purely mathematical composite variable.

**100. Panchadha Maitri (Five-Fold Relationship) in Transit**
Combine natural friendship with temporal (Tatkalika) friendship — planets within 5 signs of each other are temporary friends. This gives a dynamic, transit-only relationship matrix updated daily.

**101. Vargottama Transit (Same Sign in Rasi & Navamsa)**
A planet in the 1st, 5th, or 9th pada-set positions such that its Navamsa sign equals its Rasi sign. Vargottama planets deliver amplified results — test slow-planet Vargottama windows for trend acceleration.

**102. Pushkara Navamsa Transit**
Specific auspicious navamsas within each sign (e.g., 21°40′–25° Aries, 14°–17°20′ Taurus, per the Pushkara table). Planets here become nourishing/prosperous — test as growth markers.

**103. Pushkara Bhaga (Auspicious Single Degrees)**
One specific degree per sign (Aries 21°, Taurus 14°, Gemini 24°, Cancer 7°, Leo 21°, Virgo 14°, Libra 24°, Scorpio 7°, Sagittarius 21°, Capricorn 14°, Aquarius 24°, Pisces 7°). Very high-resolution, testable crossing dates.

**104. Mrityu Bhaga (Death Degrees)**
A table assigning a fatal degree to each planet in each sign (e.g., Sun 20° Aries, Moon 26° Taurus, Mars 19° Gemini, etc.). Planets crossing their Mrityu Bhaga are classic destruction triggers — high-precision daily flags.

**105. Rasi Sandhi (Sign-Junction Zones)**
The last 1° and first 1° of every sign — a transitional void. Test slow planets (Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu) in sandhi for indecision, and fast planets for micro-reversals.

**106. Ati-Charana / Amsha-Level Dignity (D-9, D-10, D-12 of Transiting Planets)**
Since divisional charts are computed purely from longitude, you can derive the transiting planet's Navamsa (D-9), Dashamsa (D-10, career/commerce), and Dwadashamsa (D-12) sign with zero natal input. Test slow-planet Dashamsa sign-changes against market regime.

**107. Shashtiamsha (D-60) Transit Zones**
The 0.5° divisions with 60 named amshas per sign, half auspicious (Amrita, Chandra, Indu, Kaala-rupa...) and half inauspicious (Kaala, Vishaghna, Kulaghna, Mrityu, Rudra, Yama...). Ultra-high-resolution flags for fast-planet backtests.

**108. Trimshamsha (D-30) Malefic/Benefic Zones**
The 30-part division ruled only by Mars, Saturn, Jupiter, Mercury, Venus, with different orders in odd and even signs. Test planets in Mars/Saturn trimshamshas for adverse days.

**109. Hora (D-2 — Sun/Moon Half-Signs)**
Each sign splits into a Solar and Lunar hora. Wealth-related by classical definition; test the composite count of planets in Solar vs. Lunar horas.

**110. Drekkana (D-3) Transit**
Each sign's three 10° decanates. Test slow planets by decanate for finer regime resolution than whole-sign transits.

**111. Combust-Adjacent Dignity: Ati-Bala / Ati-Nirbala Composite**
Construct a daily numerical Shadbala-like score using only natal-free components — Sthana Bala (dignity), Dig Bala (directional strength by sign), Cheshta Bala (retrograde/speed strength), Naisargika Bala (fixed), and Paksha Bala (Moon phase). Regress the aggregate against index returns.

**112. Dig Bala (Directional Strength) in Transit**
Jupiter/Mercury are strong in the east-point signs, Sun/Mars in the south-point, Saturn in the west-point, Moon/Venus in the north-point. In a natal-free frame, use the sign-based version: Jupiter & Mercury strongest in Pisces/Gemini axis logic, etc.

**113. Cheshta Bala (Motional Strength)**
Computed from a planet's speed relative to its mean speed — maximum when retrograde, minimum when at maximum direct speed. This is a pure ephemeris variable requiring no chart.

## SECTION E — RETROGRADE MOTION (VAKRI GATI)

**114. Vakri (Retrograde) Status of Each Planet**
Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn retrograde periodically; Rahu/Ketu are perpetually retrograde. Test each planet's retrograde window separately against index returns and volatility.

**115. Mercury Retrograde (Budha Vakri)**
Occurs 3–4 times yearly for ~21 days. Classically associated with reversal of contracts, miscommunication, data errors, and trading mistakes — the single most-cited retrograde in financial astrology.

**116. Mercury Retrograde Shadow Zone (Pre & Post Retrograde)**
The period when Mercury traverses the retrograde degree-range before turning (pre-shadow) and again after turning direct (post-shadow). Test the full ~2-month "storm" rather than just the 21 days.

**117. Mercury Stationary Retrograde & Stationary Direct Points**
The exact dates Mercury's speed hits zero. These are the sharpest turning-point candidates — test ±3 trading days around each station.

**118. Venus Retrograde (Shukra Vakri)**
Occurs ~every 19 months for ~42 days. Classically linked to reversal in value, luxury goods, currencies, and pleasure-sector revaluation — a strong candidate for testing market tops.

**119. Venus Retrograde Descending vs. Ascending (Morning/Evening Star Phase)**
Venus retrograde occurs around its inferior conjunction with the Sun, switching from evening star to morning star. Classical Vedic texts assign completely different price effects to Venus rising in the east vs. west.

**120. Mars Retrograde (Mangala Vakri)**
Occurs ~every 25.5 months for ~60–80 days. Associated with frustrated aggression, energy-sector reversal, and sudden violent corrections.

**121. Jupiter Retrograde (Guru Vakri)**
~4 months every 13 months. Classically linked to reversal of expansion, credit contraction, and re-evaluation of valuations/optimism.

**122. Saturn Retrograde (Shani Vakri)**
~4.5 months every 12.5 months. Linked to delayed karma, structural re-testing, and grinding declines or grinding recoveries.

**123. Stationary Points of All Planets (Sthambhana)**
Every planet's zero-speed moment. Aggregate all stations of all planets into a single "station density" series and test for turning-point clustering.

**124. Ati-Chara Gati (Accelerated / Swift Motion)**
When a planet moves faster than its mean daily motion. Classically indicates hurried, unstable outcomes — test fast-Mercury and fast-Mars phases for momentum bursts.

**125. Manda Gati (Slow Motion)**
Sub-mean speed without retrogression. Indicates delay, stagnation, and slow grinding markets.

**126. Kutila, Vikala, Anuvakra, Vakra, Mandatara, Sama, Chara, Atichara, Shighra (Ashta-Gati / Eight Motions)**
Classical Vedic eight-fold classification of planetary motion states based on speed relative to mean. Each has distinct predicted results — a natural categorical variable for backtesting.

**127. Retrograde Planet in Exaltation (Vakri-Uccha Paradox)**
Classical debate: a retrograde exalted planet behaves as debilitated, and a retrograde debilitated planet as exalted. Test both interpretations empirically — this is a genuinely resolvable question with backtest data.

**128. Retrograde Crossing of Its Own Prior Degree (Triple Transit)**
When a planet crosses a specific degree three times (direct, retrograde, direct again). Test whether the *third* crossing marks the definitive market turn.

**129. Simultaneous Multiple Retrogrades (Retrograde Pile-Up)**
Count of retrograde planets on each day (0–5, excluding nodes). Test index performance stratified by retrograde count — a clean, purely numeric variable.

**130. Retrograde Rahu/Ketu Direct-Motion Anomaly**
The True Node occasionally turns briefly direct (unlike the Mean Node). Test True-Node direct-motion windows as anomaly flags.

## SECTION F — COMBUSTION (ASTANGATA) & HELIACAL PHENOMENA

**131. Astangata (Combustion) — General Concept**
When a planet is too close to the Sun to be visible, it is "burnt" and loses its power to deliver results. Purely a function of angular distance from the Sun — perfectly natal-free.

**132. Combustion Orbs (Classical Values)**
Moon 12°, Mars 17°, Mercury 14° (direct) / 12° (retrograde), Jupiter 11°, Venus 10° (direct) / 8° (retrograde), Saturn 15°. Test each planet's combustion window separately.

**133. Deep Combustion (Within 3° of the Sun)**
The most intense burning. Test as maximum-weakness signature for the affected planet's significations (e.g., Venus deeply combust → luxury/financial sector weakness).

**134. Cazimi / Sun-Heart Conjunction (Within 17′ of the Sun)**
Tajika/Hellenistic concept where a planet in the "heart of the Sun" is empowered rather than destroyed. Rare and precisely datable — worth isolating.
